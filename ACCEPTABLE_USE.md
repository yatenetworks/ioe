# Acceptable Use

IOE AI Env Installer and its public templates are intended for lawful, self-hosted, and authorized environments.

## Allowed use

You may use this repository to:

- review and improve AI application environment template standards
- contribute documentation and low-risk examples
- test template structure on systems you own or are authorized to manage
- discuss safe lifecycle behavior for local preview work

## Prohibited use

Do not use IOE, its templates, examples, or scripts to:

- distribute or operate malware, ransomware, or spyware
- run phishing, fraud, or impersonation campaigns
- gain unauthorized access to systems, accounts, or data
- steal credentials, tokens, API keys, or personal information
- send spam or bulk unsolicited messages
- bypass security controls, licensing limits, or usage policies without permission
- misrepresent this project as an official product of another organization

## Templates and examples

Templates and examples in this repository must:

- target environments you control or are explicitly authorized to manage
- document ports, data paths, and environment variables clearly
- avoid hardcoded secrets and unsafe defaults
- avoid encouraging exposure of internal services to the public Internet by default

Contributors must not submit templates designed for abuse, credential harvesting, or covert data exfiltration.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove content or reject contributions that violate this policy.

Users are responsible for how they apply project materials on their own systems.
