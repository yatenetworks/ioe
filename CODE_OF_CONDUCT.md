# Code of Conduct

This project welcomes constructive contributions to IOE AI Env Installer and its public template standards.

## Expected behavior

Contributors and participants should:

- stay respectful and professional
- give clear, actionable feedback
- focus on documentation, templates, examples, and safe tooling improvements
- assume good intent when reviewing technical disagreements

## Unacceptable behavior

The following are not allowed:

- harassment, threats, or personal attacks
- discriminatory or abusive language
- deliberate disruption of issues or pull requests
- posting malicious links, malware, or exploit instructions
- sharing private keys, credentials, or other people's personal data

## Maintainer response

Maintainers may close, lock, or reject issues and pull requests that violate this policy or create unnecessary risk for the project or its users.

For repeated or serious abuse, maintainers may restrict participation.

## Scope

This policy applies to project repositories, issues, pull requests, and related public collaboration spaces managed for this project.

## Contact

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer. Do not post sensitive personal details in public issues.
