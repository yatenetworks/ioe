# Contributor Terms

By contributing to this repository, you confirm the following.

## Your rights

You have the right to submit the contribution, or you have permission from the rights holder to submit it under the project license.

## License grant

You agree that your contributions may be used, modified, and distributed under the same license as the project, together with other contributions already in the repository.

## What you must not submit

Do not submit:

- employer or client confidential material without written permission
- third-party code or assets you are not allowed to redistribute
- private keys, API tokens, passwords, or real `.env` files
- personal data or production user content
- content copied from restricted documentation or proprietary systems without authorization

## Accuracy

Describe what your contribution does. Do not hide destructive behavior, network calls, or third-party downloads inside unrelated changes.

## DCO

Commits should include a `Signed-off-by` line as described in [DEVELOPER_CERTIFICATE_OF_ORIGIN.md](DEVELOPER_CERTIFICATE_OF_ORIGIN.md).

If you cannot sign off on a change, do not submit it.
